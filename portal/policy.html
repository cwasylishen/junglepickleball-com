Booking and cancellation policy

Bookings can be made up to 60 days ahead for members and 30 days ahead for non-members. Any price is paid online by card through the club's Stripe checkout before the booking is confirmed. Account credit applies first. Cancel more than 48 hours before your start time and the full amount returns as credit on your account for a future booking. Cancel within 48 hours and the booking is non-refundable. Credits do not expire unless the club says otherwise.
